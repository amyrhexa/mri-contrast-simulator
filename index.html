Model, assumptions and sources

Mz(t) = M0(1 − e−t/T1)    Mxy(t) = Mxy(0) e−t/T2

Common assumptions: ideal RF pulses, single-exponential relaxation, tissue at steady state over repeated TRs, all times in ms. Neglected: noise, flow, diffusion, magnetisation transfer, B1/B0 inhomogeneity (except through T2′), partial-volume and k-space effects. Each equation applies only to its own sequence. For SE, FSE and IR the T2 curve is the envelope of the echo amplitudes.

Tissue parameters used

About these values. Sourced: T1 of white matter and grey matter at 1.5, 3 and 7 T are the MPRAGE results of Wright et al. 2008 (four healthy subjects, mean ± SD as printed in the abstract); CSF T1 is 4.3 ± 0.2 s from Rooney et al. 2007, which did not find a field dependence for ventricular CSF. These two studies use different methods and tissue definitions, and other studies report different values (for example Wansapura et al. 1999 give 832 ms for white matter and 1331 ms for grey matter at 3 T, noticeably lower for grey matter than the 1607 ms used here), so the T1 values carry a method uncertainty beyond the printed SD. Not sourced (illustrative): all T2 values, fat T1, and every proton density. No in-vivo T2 values at the three fields could be verified for this revision; the 3 T white-matter and grey-matter T2 values that were present before are kept as illustrative because the Wansapura abstract text assigns 80 ms to grey matter and 110 ms to white matter, which is the reverse of the order used here and could not be reconciled from the abstract alone. Tissue-specific T2′ could not be justified from the sources found, so one illustrative T2′ is still applied to all tissues. T2′ is a relaxation time constant; R2′ = 1/T2′ and R2* = R2 + R2′. Proton densities are relative assumptions (CSF = 1). Bibliographic details of the entries marked "carried over" below are unchanged from the previous version of this page and were not re-verified.
